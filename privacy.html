Privacy Policy

gog-cli is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • Google user data accessed through the app is used only to perform commands the owner runs on their own computer.
  • Data and OAuth tokens are stored only on that computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
